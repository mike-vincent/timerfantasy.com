## Facts

- Set a timer by dragging on an analog clock face, or set an end time with End At mode.
- Preset buttons for 60, 45, 30, 20, 15, 10, and 5 minutes.
- 14 clock face scales from 60 seconds to 96 hours, with Auto Zoom as time runs down.
- Unlimited simultaneous timers, each with its own label, color, alarm sound, and loop setting.
- Menu bar countdown with controls to pause, resume, stop, and dismiss alarms.
- iCloud sync of timers and settings across Macs signed into the same iCloud account.
- Siri and Shortcuts support, for example "Start a 10 minute timer in Timer Fantasy".
- Privacy: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
- Made by Mike Vincent: https://mikevincent.dev/
